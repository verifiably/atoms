bind_project_volume runs certify_sqlite_wal (two child processes) on every bind. A science test fixture binds 50 times per world build: 2.7 s of an 11 s build. Caching a successful certification per (device, mount) per process would remove it; decide whether that weakens the durability guarantee (a remount between binds). Filed from sci-5937be.
