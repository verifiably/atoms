Raised in the second guided tour (vdocs-e4318e, 2026-10-24): no task in atoms, nodes or beliefs tracks direct nodes adoption. The authority design §12.2 defers it: nodes keeps normative Python/TypeScript parity while atoms is Python-only, so 'portable nodes should not depend on it until a language-neutral execution seam exists (a serialized spec plus out-of-process executor is the plausible shape)'; Plan B (§14) says direct nodes adoption 'waits on' that seam. Today beliefs combines nodes and atoms at its composition root, so beliefs' writes are covered; a program writing through nodes alone, in either language, does not get atoms' guarantees. The atoms abstract no longer mentions the gap since atoms-dd5a5e (its deferred minor); only design §1 and §12.2 keep it. Scoping question: is there a nodes consumer other than beliefs that needs crash-safe writes, and if so, is the seam (serialized spec plus out-of-process executor) worth a design?
